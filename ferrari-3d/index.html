    02Overture

    Born at nine thousand revolutions.

    Nine thousand rpm. 127 horsepower per litre. A flat-plane crank that screams instead of rumbles. The 458 was engineered around a single sensation — the moment the needle stops climbing and the horizon starts arriving.

    03Design

    Sculpted by air.

    Front winglets that flex at speed to cut drag. A shape with no spoiler, because the body is the spoiler. Every surface earns its place in the wind tunnel.
